## First draft — the member roadmap

### Right now
- **Better, faster support.** We've rebuilt how we help you — quicker replies, and
  we're going back through older unresolved issues to make them right.

### Coming soon
- **A rebuilt app.** Faster, cleaner, and better looking — the same training you know,
  in a much better experience.
- **Smoother, higher-quality streaming.** An optimized experience with better video
  quality throughout.
- **The community forum is back.** Reconnect with the GymnasticBodies community.
- **You help decide what's next.** Suggest features and vote on ideas — a roadmap the
  community shapes together.

### A better training experience
- **A more dynamic level system** — progressions that adapt per workout and across
  every section of the app.
- **Better organization and a more visual interface** — everything easier to find and
  nicer to use.
- **Clearer progress and milestone tracking** — always see where you are, what you've
  achieved, and what's next.
- **Thousands more training videos** added to the library.

### Train for your goals and your space
- **Targeted workouts for specific body parts, injuries, and recovery** — routines to
  strengthen or rehab exactly what you need.
- **Workouts organized around the equipment you have** — find sessions that fit your
  setup, wherever you train.
- **Equipment recommendations and links** — know what you need and where to get it.

### New ways to train (new membership levels)
- **Basic** — full access to the training.
- **Concierge** — everything in Basic, plus personal feedback on your questions and
  your form videos from a qualified member of our team.
- **Interactive** — everything in Concierge, plus live events.

### On the horizon (exploring)
- Live workouts and events
- A mobile app experience
- Member profiles and social features
- A new forum
